## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to 6 unused days can be carried over into the next year; any balance above 6 days lapses on 31 March.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely from anywhere in India. Working from outside India for more than 30 days in a year requires written approval from the People team, because of tax and data-residency obligations.

## Equipment

Each new employee receives a laptop and a one-time home-office allowance of INR 25,000. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of joining to claim the allowance.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses, books and conference tickets. Unspent budget does not roll over.

## Incident response

Production incidents are classified as SEV1, SEV2 or SEV3. A SEV1 incident requires the on-call engineer to acknowledge the page within 10 minutes and post a status update every 30 minutes until resolved. A written postmortem is due within 5 working days of any SEV1.
